# Privacy Policy — Spartan Launcher

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This policy describes how the Spartan Launcher application ("the App", "we") handles user data. The App is a minimalist, privacy-first home screen and digital-wellbeing tool.

## Data we collect

Short version: **nothing leaves your device.** The App does not use the network for data collection, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

To provide its core functionality the App reads the following data **on-device only**:

- **App usage (Usage Access + Accessibility)**: the list of apps you open and for how long. Used exclusively to enforce your chosen daily time limits, show your screen-time report, and (with your permission) show a "time limit reached" screen. This data stays locally in the App's private storage.
- **Notifications (Notification Access)**: the apps your notifications come from. Used exclusively to let you mute notification sounds for chosen apps and to quiet notifications during a Focus session. Notification *contents* are not read, stored, or transmitted.
- **Accessibility data**: the name of the currently focused app, so the App can enforce blocking and time limits. No screen contents or input data are accessed or recorded.
- **Your settings**: blocked apps, schedules, focus sessions, labels, and theme choices. Stored locally via Android DataStore.

## Permissions

All special-access permissions (Usage Access, Accessibility, Notification Access, Display over other apps) are requested in-app with a clear explanation, are optional, and can be revoked at any time in system Settings. Features are disabled until the corresponding permission is granted.

## Third parties

None. We do not share, sell, or transmit any data to any third party.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data resides in the App's private storage. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes it. Choose "Erase data" in the App's screen-time page to reset usage records.

## Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and contains no child-directed content.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when the updated policy is published in the app's store listing.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the store listing's support email.
